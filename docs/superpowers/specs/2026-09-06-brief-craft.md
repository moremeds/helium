## (b) Rules extracted

1. **One idea per issue, named in the title.** (Marks) A title that could head tomorrow's brief is not a title.
2. **Depth goes to exactly one item; everything else is a line.** (Morning Bid: ~400–450-word essay, then flat lists.) Equal-length sections tell the reader nothing was ranked.
3. **Lead with what's new, then why it matters, in that order, in the first two sentences.** (Axios) Setup-first loses the reader and gets trimmed.
4. **Order by importance so the bottom can be cut blind.** (Inverted pyramid) The word cap must never be able to delete the conclusion.
5. **Bracket-test every clause; if it does no work, cut it.** (Zinsser + Orwell iii) Applies hardest to hedges true under every outcome.
6. **Charts/tables support the thesis, they are not the thesis.** (Chartbook) Coverage tables and level tables leave the prose budget entirely.
7. **Publish the same small set of objects every day, at fixed names.** (SpotGamma: five levels, every session) Stability is what makes a change legible.
8. **State invalidation as a named level plus "sustained through it".** (SpotGamma) A view without a price that kills it is not a view.
9. **Attach a base rate to any range claim.** (SpotGamma 76%/65%) Confidence words without a frequency are decoration.
10. **复盘 counts before it judges.** (新浪 复盘 sheet) Fixed counters first; narrative only after the counters exist.
11. **A phase label needs a numeric trigger.** (情绪周期 四阶段) "回暖" means 涨停>30家, not a feeling.
12. **Every entry sets a checkable goal; the next entry scores it.** (Steenbarger 2019) This is the whole loop: yesterday's "what I'll check" is today's first paragraph.
13. **Process review and P/L review are different documents.** (Steenbarger 2014) Mixing them anchors judgement to outcome.

## (c) Daily template — "One Thing"

**Total model-written prose ≤ 420 words.** Tables, levels, coverage and deltas are renderer output and cost zero words.

**Headline.** ≤ 14 words, one clause, exactly one number and its direction, no colon, no clause that would be true tomorrow. Summary lede (rule 3, 4).

**§1 THE ONE THING — 180 words.** Four beats in order: what moved and by how much (≤ 40); why this and not something else — the renderer hands the model the phrase "largest normalised move of the session, N× its 20-session median" (≤ 25); the mechanism, in cause→effect sentences (≤ 65); who is hurt, ranked (≤ 50).

**§2 WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND — 40 words.** Must contain (i) a named series or level, (ii) a threshold, (iii) a horizon. Template: _"X sustained through/above L for N sessions kills this; a print of P on DATE resolves it."_ No threshold → the section is dropped and the brief is flagged, not padded (rule 8).

**§3 WHAT I'LL CHECK TOMORROW — 3 bullets × 15 words.** Each names a tool-observable series and a number. These are stored; tomorrow's §1 opens by scoring them (rule 12).

**§4 EVERYTHING ELSE — ≤ 5 lines × 12 words, one fact + one number each.** Only channels scoring ≥ 1.0. No prose, no connectives.

**§5 POSITIONS / CANDIDATES.** Renderer-owned table. Model writes only a ≤ 25-word rationale per row.

**Coverage / as-of / lag.** Renderer footer, machine-formatted, outside the prose budget and outside the model's reach (rule 6). This also removes the entire class of meta-leak the current gate keeps catching.

### How "the one thing" is chosen — code, not model

Renderer computes, per channel, `score = |today's move| / median(|move|, trailing 20 sessions)` from stored history, and ranks:

| #   | Channel                        | Move measured                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0   | Position invalidation breached | hard override — always wins                                                                     |
| 1   | Rates                          | Δ10Y bp; Δ2s10s bp                                                                              |
| 2   | Policy                         | Δ hike/cut probability, pp                                                                      |
| 3   | Credit                         | Δ HY OAS bp                                                                                     |
| 4   | Vol                            | Δ VIX pts                                                                                       |
| 5   | Dealer positioning             | spot crossing zero-gamma; call/put wall break                                                   |
| 6   | Flow                           | net premium sign flip; \|net premium\|                                                          |
| 7   | Divergence pair                | two channels moving against their stored sign convention — score = min of the pair's two scores |
| 8   | Dated event landing today      | fixed score 2.0                                                                                 |

Winner = highest score, ties broken by the # column. **Threshold: the top score must be ≥ 2.0.** The model receives the ranked list with pre-formatted numbers and writes §1 about row 1 only. It may not re-rank.

**Numbers.** Every figure in prose is copied character-for-character from a tool string or from a renderer-computed delta string. The model never subtracts, converts or rounds — 8 of 11 model-computed numbers were wrong on 2026-09-03, which is why the ranking is arithmetic the renderer owns.

**Nothing-happened day (top score < 2.0).** Mode switches to _persistence_: the one thing becomes the longest-running unchanged condition, with the streak length computed by the renderer ("HY OAS inside 6bp for 14 sessions"). §1 shrinks to 90 words, §4 to 3 lines, §2 and §3 stay mandatory. **The quiet-day brief is shorter, never padded** (rule 5). A missing source is never the one thing and never appears in prose.

## (d) 复盘 template — 5 / 10 / 21 trading days

Counters first, judgement after (rule 10). All three windows open with a renderer-built count sheet; the model writes only the pattern paragraph.

**5 days — process only. P/L is forbidden in this document.**
Counters: one-thing hit rate (was the named channel still top-ranked 1 and 3 sessions later?); §3 checks scored yes/no/not-observed; invalidation levels breached vs. honoured; word budget overruns; coverage gaps by layer.
Model writes ≤ 200 words answering one question: _which channel did I keep over-reading, and which did I keep missing?_

**10 days — regime.** Counters: how many distinct "one things"; longest run on one channel; count of divergence-pair wins; phase label with its numeric trigger (borrowing 情绪周期's discipline — a label needs a number, rule 11).
Model writes ≤ 250 words: _did the regime change, and did I say so before or after it changed?_ Still no P/L.

**21 days — outcome, and the only window that shows P/L.** One option cycle, matching the 21-DTE review the desk already runs. Counters: realised P/L by structure, win rate, expectancy per unit risked, and — printed alongside, never below — the 5-day process stats for the same window, so outcome is never read alone (rule 13). Sample size is printed with the expectancy; under ~30 closed trades the document must print "sample too small to score edge" instead of a verdict.

## (f) What the current personas contradict

1. **60-word cap on every section** (`editor`, `regime-analyst`) makes the most important thing the same size as the least. Rules 1, 2.
2. **"At most FIVE sections… choose the five that matter"** hands ranking to the model. The ranking is arithmetic over history; it belongs to the renderer.
3. **`regime-analyst`'s fixed tag menu** (CAUSE / DIVERGENCE / REACTION FUNCTION / BEAT-AND-RAISE) writes the day to the template. Rule 1.
4. **"2Y/10Y is a MANDATORY datapoint every day"** guarantees rates prose on days rates did nothing — the opposite of channel ranking, and it produced "Rates are the story" in the source brief.
5. **No macro invalidation field anywhere.** `Invalidation` exists only per-candidate. Rule 8.
6. **`overnight` is 5 unranked observations with no thresholds and nothing scores them tomorrow.** Rule 12 — this is the loop's missing half.
7. **"Say what CHANGED, not what IS"** with no code-supplied delta: the model must difference, which is exactly the arithmetic that failed 8/11 times.
8. **Layer Coverage occupies a prose section** inside the five-section budget and is the direct cause of the recurring `meta-leak` refusals. Rule 6 moves it out of prose entirely.
9. **No quiet-day mode**, so a low-information day is written at full length. Rules 5, and the 2026-09-03 run's four-of-four over-budget sections.
