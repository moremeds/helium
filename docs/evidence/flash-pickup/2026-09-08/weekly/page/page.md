Outlook

Next week’s base case is selective continuation rather than an index-wide risk-on move. Hardware leadership is confirmed if Memory/Storage, Foundry and Computer/GPU continue outperforming SPY. It is invalidated if those groups surrender relative strength, especially alongside a rebound in Semi-Cap/EDA. The existing split matters because it shows investors distinguishing between hardware beneficiaries rather than buying the entire technology chain.

Policy remains the macro hinge. The 2026-09-16 hike probability stands at 55.7% while DGS10 is 4.77. If hike pricing extends and DGS10 reverses above 4.79, judgement says pressure on Software/SaaS and Cloud/Hyperscaler should persist. If hike probability falls below 50.7 while DGS10 remains below 4.77, the strongest reverse risk is a rebound in those laggards.

Credit and volatility should confirm any broader deterioration. BAMLH0A0HYM2 above 2.65 together with VIX above 15.2 would provide stronger risk-off evidence; continued tight credit and subdued volatility would argue that weakness remains rotational. SPY at 769.55 stands above the 768.35 gamma flip. Holding above that level supports an orderly tape, while a close below it alongside rising VIX would weaken the setup.

ADBE and ORCL are the direct tests of weak software and cloud leadership. Reactions exceeding their respective 6.9333% and 9.8619% implied moves could determine whether the divergence broadens or begins reversing; IV does not determine direction.

Agriculture leadership can persist if DBA, MOS, NTR and DE maintain relative strength and the operator-checked ONI and grain-price premises remain intact. The medium-term thesis fails if ONI is below +0.5 for two consecutive months or the instruments underperform SPY by 10% over 60 sessions.
