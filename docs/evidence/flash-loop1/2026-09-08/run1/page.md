The week was a hardware-versus-software split, not a broad-tape move and not a risk-off week

For the week ended 2026-09-04, SPY returned +0.1% (rotation, benchmarkReturns w1) — essentially flat. That flatness is the story's disguise: beneath it, silicon-and-metal leadership diverged sharply from software, so this was not a broad rally (the index barely moved), not a risk-off week (VIXCLS eased to 14.32 from a prior 15.20, a -0.88-pt move as of 2026-09-03; high-yield spread BAMLH0A0HYM2 tightened to 2.65 from 2.66), and not a rate-driven repricing (DGS10 slipped only -2 bp to 4.77 as of 2026-09-03). Outlook

The clearest question into next week is whether the hardware-over-software split is a durable AI-capacity rotation or a one-week dispersion that mean-reverts. The bull case rests on breadth: Memory/Storage (+7.5% vs SPY), Computer/GPU (+5.7%) and Foundry (+5.3%) all moved with every member participating (4/4, 6/6, 4/4), which is harder to dismiss as single-name noise than a narrow move would be. It persists if those excesses stay positive in the next rotation read; it invalidates if Cybersecurity's -7.0% and Software/SaaS's -4.8% excesses snap back, which would recast this week as an unwind. Reverse risk: a sharp software recovery would pull the flat index the other way with the same tape.

The macro backdrop is quiet and gives the rotation room. DGS10 at 4.77 (-2 bp), high-yield at 2.65 (-1 bp) and VIXCLS at 14.32 (-0.88 pts) are all calm as of 2026-09-03 — but I read these as observations, not evidence of complacency or of what is priced. The one live policy datum is the 9/16 hike probability at 55.7 (as of 2026-09-04); the frame excludes it from ranking for lack of a prior, so I can quote the level but not a direction of travel. That FOMC decision is 2026-09-16 — later context, beyond the coming seven days, not a next-week pivot.

The energy and ag-input leadership is the most persistent feature: el-nino-ag +6.5% excess this week stacks on +13.3% (4w) and +11.6% (12w), and XLE echoes it across all three windows. Multi-horizon consistency argues for continuation over a one-week spike. It persists if crude holds its bid and the basket keeps positive weekly excess; it fades if a dated ONI reading comes in below +0.5 or the basket underperforms SPY, which is the theme's own armed kill condition. Reverse risk: a crude reversal would hit XLE and the ag complex together.

Into the ADBE print on 2026-09-10, the software-lagging tape is the frame for a name whose sector fell -4.8% vs SPY this week; the read-through to the wider Software/SaaS group matters more than the single reaction. Watch whether one soft print deepens the group's relative weakness or whether the sector was already discounting it.
