The week markets sold software and bought hardware, not risk-off, not a rate scare

The five sessions to 2026-09-04 were a hardware-over-software rotation inside a flat tape, not a broad risk-off (SPY returned just +0.1% for the week and VIXCLS fell to 14.32, −0.88 pts, asOf 2026-09-03) and not a rates-driven repricing (DGS10 slipped only −2 bp to 4.77, asOf 2026-09-03). The dispersion was in equities, not across asset classes.

Hardware led on a dated earnings beat

Computer/GPU was the strongest equity block, +5.7% vs SPY across six of six priced members (asOf 2026-09-04). The plausible driver is dated: Dell reported after the close on 2026-09-01, inside the window, with actual EPS of 6.76 against a street mean estimate of 4.95 (Unusual Whales, fiscal quarter ending 2026-07-31; EPS basis not stated, so treat this as a directional beat, not a computed surprise). Dell was the third-ranked single name for the week at +14.77% excess vs SPY. The mechanism I'd claim is that an AI-server upside print re-rated the box-and-GPU complex broadly rather than Dell alone — Foundry moved with it, +5.3% vs SPY (four of four priced). The competing reading is pure beta or index effect; the coverage ratio (six of six priced) measures data coverage, not breadth, so it cannot confirm every member rose. Confirm: Computer/GPU excess holds positive into next Friday's rotation table. Refute: the block gives back its lead and prints negative excess vs SPY on 2026-09-11.

Memory was the single largest mover, on price alone

Memory/Storage led all sectors at +7.5% vs SPY (four of four priced, asOf 2026-09-04), and SanDisk was the top single name at +17.06% excess vs SPY. I have no dated in-window catalyst for the group: SanDisk's most recent report was 2026-08-05 (fiscal quarter ending 2026-06-30, actual EPS 38.82 vs street 34.24) — pre-window historical context, not this week's cause. So the +7.5% is an observation, not an explained event; the strength may be momentum or read-through from the hardware bid rather than fresh news. Confirm: Memory/Storage stays the top sector by weekly excess on 2026-09-11. Refute: it prints negative excess vs SPY next Friday.

Ag inputs kept the rotation crown

The el-nino-ag-2026 basket (DBA, MOS, NTR, DE) returned +6.6% for the week and led the rotation table at +6.5% excess vs SPY (asOf 2026-09-04), extending a run visible over four weeks (+13.3% excess) and twelve (+11.6% excess). Energy corroborated the reflation tilt — XLE +2.2% (w1), +11.8% four-week excess. The theme's declared triggers (NOAA ONI ≥ +0.5 for three consecutive months; CBOT corn/wheat vs 20-day) are operator-checked and I have no dated reading for either input, so I state performance, not confirmation of the weather thesis. Confirm: basket weekly excess stays positive on 2026-09-11. Refute: it turns negative vs SPY next Friday.

The soft spot was software and security

Against the hardware bid, Cybersecurity fell −7.0% vs SPY (four of four priced) and Software/SaaS −4.8% (six of six priced), with Figma the weakest single name at −16.42% excess vs SPY. I have no supplied dated catalyst for the security drop, so I do not name one; note only that this weakness is not a funding source for hardware — returns do not trace capital transfers.

Cross-asset backdrop stayed benign: credit spread BAMLH0A0HYM2 at 2.65, −1 bp (asOf 2026-09-03), and the SPY gamma-flip level ticked to 768.35 (asOf 2026-09-04T20:14:28.094000Z). Low vol and tight spreads are observations here, not evidence of complacency. Limitation: the dated close-report archive and the news feed both returned empty this run, so every claim above rests on the frame and rotation payloads, not on week-through headlines.

Outlook

The near-term question is whether the hardware-over-software split is a durable rotation or a one-print reaction to Dell's 2026-09-01 beat. The bull case is that an AI-server upside surprise re-rates Computer/GPU and Foundry as a group; the invalidation is mechanical — if those blocks give back their weekly excess vs SPY on the 2026-09-11 rotation table while Software/SaaS recovers, the move was Dell-specific, not a theme. Reverse risk: a soft Adobe print (post-close 2026-09-10, implied move 6.9333%) could deepen the software drawdown and, by sentiment, drag the broader growth complex even as hardware holds.

Memory/Storage carries the largest weekly excess but the thinnest explanation. Without a dated in-window catalyst, I treat next week as a test of whether the +7.5% was momentum that fades or the start of a memory-cycle move that needs fresh data to sustain. Watch whether the group keeps top-sector status on 2026-09-11; a reversal to negative excess would confirm it was a crowded one-week trade.

The el-nino-ag basket and energy remain the cleanest medium-horizon leadership: both show positive one-, four- and twelve-week excess. Persistence depends on the reflation tape holding — a firmer dollar or a crude reversal would be the first tell against it. The theme's own kill conditions (ONI below +0.5 two months running, or 10% SPY underperformance over 60 sessions) are not met on any dated reading I hold, so the basket stays live but unconfirmed on weather grounds.

On macro, the path repriced hawkish in the background: the 9/16 hike probability sat at 55.7 (asOf 2026-09-04), but that is later context, not a next-seven-days event, and it had no prior observation to measure a weekly change against. With DGS10 at 4.77 and the HY spread at 2.65, the bond and credit markets are not signaling stress; the risk to the equity rotation is therefore idiosyncratic and earnings-driven next week, not a rates or credit shock — unless the front-end repricing (which I cannot size, DGS2 not ingested) forces long yields higher. The observable that would flip the benign read: DGS10 breaking materially above 4.77 or the HY spread widening off 2.65 on next Friday's prints.
