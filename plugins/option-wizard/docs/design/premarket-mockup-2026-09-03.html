Macro read
Three things worth saying

Rates are the first cause. The 10Y is 4.788% and the 30Y 5.261%, while the 2Y at 4.375% has barely moved. That is a bear-steepener: what is being repriced is term premium and a firmer policy path, not a cut. Ranked by cash-flow duration, the damage runs in this order. First, the longest-duration, profitless story equity — the ARM, APP, OKLO, ASTS, CRWV cohort, where value is almost entirely terminal. Second, big-cap secular growth in QQQ and SMH, where the multiple is the position. Third, rate-sensitive REITs and utilities (XLRE, XLU) and homebuilders (TOL). Short-duration cash-flow compounders — XLP, KO, MCD, WMT — are hurt least. A 10Y at 4.79% against a 30Y at 5.26% is the single most important fact on the tape going into a payrolls week.

The most anomalous divergence is gold. Gold is $4,427.62, up 0.90%, in the same tape as a 10Y real yield of 2.44% that has itself climbed from 2.32% on 25 August. Gold rising while real yields rise is the anomaly; the bar it faced was "real carry is getting more expensive, fade me," and it cleared that bar anyway. Attribute it to the expectations bar, not to the growth cycle. With the futures path now pricing hike risk and the dollar soft at 99.215 (−0.36), gold is trading as a policy-mistake and debasement hedge rather than as a real-rate instrument. That is a different buyer from the one pricing the 10Y.

The "beat-and-raise that closed down" tag is left unassigned today. The label requires three things verified together — the beat, the raise, and the red close. This run received no per-name earnings prints or closing changes for the watchlist, and the flow and tide data is the prior session, frozen. Rather than name a candidate on pattern alone, the tag is recorded as unassigned. AVGO comes closest — a revenue and EPS beat — but its Q4 guide of ~$34.8B missed the ~$35.0B consensus, so the tag stays unassigned.

Reverse risk

The base case is a bear-steepener that grinds duration equity lower, so the risk that deserves its own space is a dovish resolution that squeezes exactly the cohort path A is short. If payrolls disappoint enough to price the September hike back out — the 2Y through 4.30%, the curve bull-steepening — the violent relief rally concentrates in the most-shorted terminal-value names: ARM, APP, OKLO, ASTS, CRWV. Prior-session positioning closed net call positive, which means the pain trade is up, not down, and lightly hedged books get run over on a downside-yield surprise. Two things compound it. The 10Y real yield we are leaning on was last observed on 1 September, two days stale, so the anchor may already have moved. And gold rising alongside rising real yields is itself a warning that the rate-instrument read of this tape is not the only buyer in the room. A dovish print onto an already-soft dollar could turn the whole duration cohort green in a session, and frozen prior-session flow tells you nothing about how books are hedged into 12:30Z tomorrow.
